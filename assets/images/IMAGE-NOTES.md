## Image quality update
- Product, IoT, award, and NovaBinQ images were enlarged for high-density displays and lightly sharpened.
- Homepage activity cards use the original full-size Massdyna image URLs rather than 300x400 or 600x800 thumbnail variants.
- Existing large photographic WebP assets were retained at native resolution and re-encoded at high quality.
- `civio-iot-concept.webp` is a newly generated illustrative concept image showing a CIVIO-style waste processor connected to abstract dashboard and mobile screens. It is not a photograph of a specific production model, and its interface is not a live system capture or source of actual operating data.
